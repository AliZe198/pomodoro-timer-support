# PomoFloat Support

[English](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/) · [简体中文](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/support-zh-hans.html) · [繁體中文](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/support-zh-hant.html)

PomoFloat is a native Mac focus timer for macOS 14 or later. It supports English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Settings, tasks, and focus records stay on your Mac; no account is required.

## Start and choose a view

Choose 5 minutes to 4 hours of focus time; breaks are added separately. Use Count Up for a session without a fixed end. Settings → Appearance offers six Focus Views, five wallpaper scenes, and dark or white solid backgrounds.

## Find your timer

Closing the main window does not quit the app. Use the menu bar item to reopen it, or choose Window → Show Main Window while PomoFloat is active. The button at the top right of the main window toggles the floating timer. Settings → Floating timer offers six styles, always-on-top, and display over full-screen apps. Drag the window or its edges to move or resize it; click a compact timer to expand it.

## Tasks and saved records

Open the task list from the left of the main window. Pin it to keep it open, or click outside an unpinned panel to close it. Select a task with its arrow; use × to clear the selection. Changing tasks does not reset the timer. Pomodoro assigns time to each task as you switch; Count Up assigns the whole unsaved session to the last selected task when saved.

Completed tasks are kept for 30 days by default. Settings → Tasks also offers 7 days or Keep Forever. Removing a task does not remove its recorded focus statistics. Records do not automatically sync across devices. The App Store edition uses its own sandbox; records from a development build are not imported automatically.

## Progress, sleep, and quitting

Use History or View → Show Statistics to review your records. Click the goal duration at the bottom of the main window to change your daily goal. Focus streaks use saved time that meets the goal, excluding breaks. Unsaved Count Up time only previews progress.

Count Up pauses when the Mac sleeps and waits for you to resume; it also pauses at 24 hours. Quitting normally saves it. After an unexpected exit, it restores the latest checkpoint in a paused state. Pomodoro uses the scheduled end time and refreshes after waking. Alerts may not arrive during sleep.

## Missing sounds or notifications

In Settings → Sound, check the ambient audio selection and the separate completion sound and notification switches. Check macOS System Settings → Notifications and the Mac’s Focus mode. Denying notifications does not prevent timing. Ambient audio pauses with the timer and during breaks.

## Updates and privacy

Retail App Store installations check Apple’s catalog for updates. Settings → About shows update information; the App Store downloads and installs updates. Update actions are unavailable during a timer session, including when paused. Development and test installations do not check for retail updates. Core timing, statistics, sounds, and artwork work offline.

Read the [privacy policy](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/privacy.html).
