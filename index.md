# Pomodoro Focus Timer Support

Pomodoro Focus Timer is a native macOS focus timer with a menu bar control,
floating timer, ambient sounds, tasks, and local statistics.

## Before contacting support

- Confirm that your Mac is running macOS 14 or later.
- If the main window is closed, click the timer icon in the menu bar and choose
  **Open Main Window**.
- Notification permission can be checked in macOS System Settings.
- Your tasks and focus history are stored locally on your Mac.
