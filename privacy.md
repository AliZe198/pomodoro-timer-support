# Privacy Policy for PomoFloat

[English](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/privacy.html) · [简体中文](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/privacy-zh-hans.html) · [繁體中文](https://alize198.github.io/pomodoro-timer-support/privacy-zh-hant.html)

Last updated: October 10, 2026

PomoFloat is a Mac app that works locally on your device. The app
does not require an account.

## Information stored on your Mac

The app stores your preferences, tasks, focus sessions, and statistics on your
Mac so that they remain available when you reopen the app. The app does not
send this information to the developer or to third parties.

## Retention and local control

Completed tasks are kept for 30 days by default; you can choose 7 days or Keep Forever in Settings → Tasks. Removing a task does not remove already recorded focus statistics. Focus history remains locally until removed from the app’s stored data. There is no automatic cloud sync or account to delete. Developer-build records are not automatically imported into the App Store sandbox.

## Data collection and sharing

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
The developer does not collect your personal information through the app. Its
ambient sounds and artwork are bundled with the app and do not require streaming.

## App Store update checks

The App Store edition periodically contacts Apple's app catalog to find
available updates for your App Store region. These requests include the app's
identifier and the store region; Apple also receives the ordinary network
information needed to process a request, such as your IP address. Your tasks,
focus history, and statistics are not included. Update-check results may be
cached locally. Downloads and installation are handled by the Mac App Store.
Core timing and statistics continue to work without an internet connection.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses macOS notifications to tell you when
a timer finishes. Notification permission can be changed in macOS System
Settings.

## Public support website

Opening support or privacy links launches a browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider receives ordinary web request information. This is separate from the app’s local task and focus storage.

## Support requests

If you contact the developer outside the app, the information you choose to
include in your message is used only to respond to that request. The app does
not send support requests automatically.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's behavior changes. The current version
will be available at the same public URL. For privacy questions, email
[luzihui.yilia@gmail.com](mailto:luzihui.yilia@gmail.com).
