# Privacy Policy for Pomodoro Focus Timer

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Pomodoro Focus Timer is a Mac app that works locally on your device. The app
does not require an account.

## Information stored on your Mac

The app stores your preferences, tasks, focus sessions, and statistics on your
Mac so that they remain available when you reopen the app. The app does not
send this information to the developer or to third parties.

## Data collection and sharing

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
It does not collect or share personal information through the app. Its ambient
sounds and artwork are bundled with the app and do not require streaming.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses macOS notifications to tell you when
a timer finishes. Notification permission can be changed in macOS System
Settings.

## Support requests

If you contact the developer outside the app, the information you choose to
include in your message is used only to respond to that request. The app does
not send support requests automatically.
